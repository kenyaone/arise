💎 Why You're Not a RockKwa Nini Wewe Si JiweKwa Nini Wewe Si Jiwe

A rock can be picked up, thrown, stepped on, even cut in half — and nothing is owed to it. No one is punished for how they treat a rock.Jiwe linaweza kuokotwa, kutupwa, kukanyagwa, hata kukatwa vipande viwili — na hakuna kinachodaiwa kwalo. Hakuna anayeadhibiwa kwa jinsi analotendea jiwe.Jiwe linaweza kuokotwa, kutupwa, kukanyagwa, hata kukatwa vipande viwili — na hakuna kinachodaiwa kwalo. Hakuna anayeadhibiwa kwa jinsi analotendea jiwe.

You are not a rock. God loves you and created you with a plan for your life. Sometimes people don't treat children well — but you have rights that a rock does not, and there are real protections for how you're treated.Wewe si jiwe. Mungu anakupenda na alikuumba na mpango kwa maisha yako. Wakati mwingine wasee hawatendei watoto vizuri — lakini una rights ambazo jiwe halina, na kuna ulinzi halisi kwa jinsi unavyotendewa.

📜 Rights You Have Right NowHaki Ulizonazo Sasa HiviHaki Ulizonazo Sasa Hivi
  • You have the right to have caregivers, organisations and your government decide based on what's best for youUna haki ya kuwa na walezi, mashirika na serikali yako wakiamua kulingana na lililo bora kwakoUna right ya kuwa na walezi, mashirika na serikali yako wakiamua kulingana na lililo bora kwako
  • You have the right to be treated fairly, without discrimination for any reasonUna haki ya kutendewa kwa haki, bila ubaguzi wa aina yoyoteUna right ya kutendewa kwa haki, bila ubaguzi wa aina yoyote
  • You have the right to have a say in decisions that affect youUna haki ya kuwa na sauti katika maamuzi yanayokuhusuUna right ya kuwa na sauti katika maamuzi yanayokuhusu
  • You have the right to expect your government to provide for you economically, socially and culturallyUna haki ya kutarajia serikali yako ikutunze kiuchumi, kijamii na kiutamaduniUna right ya kutarajia serikali yako ikutunze kiuchumi, kijamii na kiutamaduni
  • You have the right to life, survival and growthUna haki ya uhai, kuishi na kukuaUna right ya uhai, kuishi na kukua
  • You have the right to privacyUna haki ya faraghaUna right ya faragha
  • You have the right to be protected from harmful or dangerous workUna haki ya kulindwa dhidi ya kazi hatari au yenye madharaUna right ya kulindwa dhidi ya kazi hatari ama yenye madhara

Sometimes a child's rights aren't respected — not always through cruelty, sometimes through hardship or unfair custom. 📘 Taken out of school to work📘 Kutolewa Shuleni Kwenda Kazini📘 Kutolewa Shuleni Kwenda KaziniA child is kept home from school to earn money for the family. This affects the right to education and protection from harmful work. A teacher, church leader, or local chief can often help the family find another way.Mtoto anawekwa nyumbani asiende shule ili apate pesa kwa familia. 📘 Left out for being poor📘 Kuachwa Nje kwa Kuwa Maskini📘 Kuachwa Nje kwa Kuwa MaskiniA child is left out of an award, a trip, or an activity because their family can't afford something. This is discrimination — a right you have regardless of your family's situation. A teacher or school administrator is usually the right person to raise this with.Mtoto anaachwa nje ya tuzo, safari, au shughuli kwa sababu familia yao haiwezi kumudu kitu fulani. Mwalimu au msimamizi wa shule kwa kawaida ndiye mtu sahihi wa kuinua jambo hili.Mtoto anaachwa nje ya tuzo, safari, ama shughuli kwa sababu familia yao haiwezi kumudu kitu fulani.
