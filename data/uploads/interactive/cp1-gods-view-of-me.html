🎓 Facilitator note · 5 min
Hold up the mirror and let each child look at themselves. Ask what they see — eyes, hair, a smile. Then tell them God sees each one of them too, but sees far more than the mirror shows: God has a plan for each life, made on purpose. Let that excite them, not just inform them.Shikilia kioo na acha kila mtoto ajiangalie. Uliza wanachokiona — macho, nywele, tabasamu. Acha hilo liwafurahishe, si kuwaeleza tu.Shikilia kioo na acha kila mtoto ajiangalie. Uliza wanachokiona — macho, nywele, tabasamu. ✝️ Made on PurposeUmeumbwa kwa MakusudiUmeumbwa kwa Makusudi

Every child in the room needs to hear, and hear again, that God loves them — and that this love does not depend on how they perform, how they look, or how anyone else treats them.Kila mtoto chumbani anahitaji kusikia, tena na tena, kwamba Mungu anampenda — na kwamba upendo huo hautegemei jinsi anavyofanya, jinsi anavyoonekana, au jinsi mtu mwingine anavyomtendea.Kila mtoto chumbani anahitaji kusikia, tena na tena, ati Mungu anampenda — na hio love haitegemei jinsi anavyofanya, jinsi anavyoonekana, ama jinsi msee mwingine anavyomtendea.
