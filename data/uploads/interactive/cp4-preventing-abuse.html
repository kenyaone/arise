🎓 Facilitator note · 5 min
Hold up the blank paper: it represents each person in the room. Hold up the marker with the cap off: sometimes people don't care about keeping the paper clean, and mark on it anyway. We can't always stop someone else's choice — but there are things we can do to protect the paper, or reduce the marks on it: put the cap on, hide it, take it to an adult. That is what today's strategies are for.Onyesha karatasi tupu: inawakilisha kila mtu chumbani. Onyesha marker bila kifuniko: wakati mwingine watu hawajali kuiweka karatasi safi, na kuchora juu yake hata hivyo. Hatuwezi kila mara kuzuia uchaguzi wa mtu mwingine — lakini kuna mambo tunayoweza kufanya kulinda karatasi, au kupunguza alama juu yake: kufunika kifuniko, kuificha, kuipeleka kwa mtu mzima. Hilo ndilo lengo la mikakati ya leo.Onyesha karatasi tupu: inawakilisha kila msee chumbani. 🔑 Private places🔑 Sehemu za Siri🔑 Private PlacesThe parts of the body that your underclothes cover. No one should ask to see or touch these, except a doctor or nurse giving needed medical care — and even then, a trusted adult should be present or aware.Sehemu za mwili zinazofunikwa na nguo za ndani. ✝️ 1 Corinthians 6:19–20 (NIV)
“Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? … Therefore honour God with your bodies.”“Au hamjui ya kuwa mwili wenu ni hekalu la Roho Mtakatifu aliye ndani yenu, mliyepewa na Mungu? … Basi mtukuzeni Mungu katika miili yenu.”“Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? … Therefore honour God with your bodies.”
A body that belongs to God is not free for anyone else to take. That is exactly why a firm “no” to an unsafe touch is not rude — it is honouring what God has entrusted to you.Mwili unaomilikiwa na Mungu si wa bure kwa mtu mwingine kuchukua. Ndiyo maana hasa “hapana” la nguvu kwa kuguswa kusiko salama si ukorofi — ni kuheshimu kile Mungu amekukabidhi.Mwili unaomilikiwa na Mungu si wa bure kwa msee mwingine kuchukua. Ndio maana hasa “hapana” la nguvu kwa touch isiyo salama si ukorofi — ni kuheshimu kile Mungu amekukabidhi.
