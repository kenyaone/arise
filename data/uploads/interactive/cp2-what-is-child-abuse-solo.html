💚 Even If This Has Happened to YouHata Kama Hili LimekutokeaHata Kama Hii Imekutokea

Nothing that has happened to you changes who you are, or how much you're worth. God's love and plan for your life stay exactly the same — even when someone else didn't treat you with love. What happened to you can leave a mark, but it doesn't decide your future, and it doesn't have to be carried alone.Hakuna kilichokutokea kinachobadilisha wewe ni nani, au thamani yako. Kilichokutokea kinaweza kuacha alama, lakini hakiamui maisha yako yajayo, wala hakihitaji kubebwa peke yako.Hakuna kilichokutokea kinachobadilisha wewe ni nani, ama thamani yako. Kilichokutokea kinaweza kuacha alama, lakini hakiamui maisha yako yajayo, wala hakihitaji kubebwa peke yako.
