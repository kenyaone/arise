🎓 Facilitator note · 5 min
This lesson may be upsetting — that is expected, not a sign something has gone wrong. Tell the group plainly that talking about abuse is how it gets dealt with, and how healing can begin. Let them know they can ask questions, make comments, or ask to talk privately at any point. Open with a short prayer: “Heavenly Father, thank You for loving us no matter what we experience. Help us express our deepest hurts, and know that You can bring healing to our lives. In Jesus' name, amen.”Somo hili linaweza kuhuzunisha — hilo linatarajiwa, si ishara kuwa kuna kilichoharibika. 🛑 If It Happens, Here's What To DoIkitokea, Hivi Ndivyo UfanyeIkitokea, Hivi Ndivyo Ufanye
  1. Say “No” and “Stop” clearlySema “Hapana” na “Acha” kwa uwaziSema “Hapana” na “Acha” kwa uwazi
  2. Get away, or call out loudly for helpOndoka, au piga kelele kutafuta msaadaOndoka, ama piga kelele kutafuta msaada
  3. Go to a trusted adult and tell them — even though it's hardNenda kwa mtu mzima wa kuaminika na umwambie — hata ikiwa ni vigumuNenda kwa trusted adult na umwambie — hata ikiwa ni vigumu
  4. Answer their questions honestly, including the hard details — this is not about protecting the person who caused the harmJibu maswali yao kwa uaminifu, ikiwa ni pamoja na maelezo magumu — hii si kuhusu kumlinda aliyesababisha madharaJibu maswali yao kwa uaminifu, ikiwemo maelezo magumu — hii si kuhusu kumlinda aliyesababisha madhara
  5. Stay somewhere safe until the situation is resolvedBaki mahali salama hadi hali itakaposhughulikiwaBaki mahali salama hadi hali itakaposhughulikiwa
  6. Let a trained counsellor help you process it, in timeAcha mshauri aliyefunzwa akusaidie kuipitia, kwa wakati wakeAcha trained counsellor akusaidie kui-process, kwa wakati wake
⚠️ Telling is never wrong⚠️ Kusema si kosa kamwe⚠️ Kusema si kosa kamweA trusted adult who hears this will not be angry with the child. Their job is to find a safe place, help make a report, and connect the child to someone who can help them heal — not to judge.Mtu mzima wa kuaminika anayesikia hili hatamkasirikia mtoto. Kazi yao ni kupata mahali salama, kusaidia kutoa report, na kumunganisha mtoto na msee anayeweza kumsaidia kupona — si kuhukumu.
