✝️ Made on PurposeUmeumbwa kwa MakusudiUmeumbwa kwa Makusudi

You need to hear, and hear again, that God loves you — and that this love does not depend on how you perform, how you look, or how anyone else treats you.Unahitaji kusikia, tena na tena, kwamba Mungu anakupenda — na kwamba upendo huo hautegemei jinsi unavyofanya, jinsi unavyoonekana, au jinsi mtu mwingine anavyokutendea.Unahitaji kusikia, tena na tena, ati Mungu anakupenda — na hio love haitegemei jinsi unavyofanya, jinsi unavyoonekana, ama jinsi msee mwingine anavyokutendea.

"So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.""Kwa hiyo Mungu akamuumba mtu kwa mfano wake, kwa mfano wa Mungu alimuumba; mwanamume na mwanamke aliwaumba.""So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them."
