🛑 If Someone Is Unkind to YouIkiwa Mtu Anakutendea VibayaIkiwa Msee Anakutendea Vibaya

Bullying, exclusion, threats, pressure from friends to do something you don't want to — none of it is okay, and none of it is your fault. Here's what actually helps:Uonevu, kutengwa, vitisho, shinikizo kutoka kwa marafiki kufanya kitu husichotaka — hakuna hata kimoja kilicho sawa, na hakuna hata kimoja kilicho kosa lako. Hivi ndivyo vinavyosaidia kweli:Bullying, kutengwa, vitisho, shinikizo kutoka kwa marafiki kufanya kitu husichotaka — hakuna hata kimoja kilicho sawa, na hakuna hata kimoja kilicho kosa lako. Hivi ndivyo vinavyosaidia kweli:

  1. Stay as calm as you can, and don't repay unkindness with more of the sameBaki mtulivu kadri uwezavyo, na usilipe ukatili kwa ukatili zaidiBaki mtulivu kadri uwezavyo, na usilipe ukatili kwa ukatili zaidi
  2. Walk away from the situation if you canOndoka kwenye hali hiyo kama unawezaOndoka kwenye hali hiyo kama unaweza
  3. Tell a trusted adult — a teacher, a parent, a relative — what's happeningMwambie mtu mzima wa kuaminika — mwalimu, mzazi, ndugu — kinachoendeleaMwambie trusted adult — teacher, mzazi, ndugu — kinachoendelea
  4. Choose your own actions and words carefully — you decide who you become, not the person being unkind to youChagua matendo na maneno yako kwa makini — wewe ndiye unayeamua utakuwa nani, si mtu anayekutendea vibayaChagua matendo na maneno yako kwa makini — wewe ndiye unaamua utakuwa nani, si msee anayekutendea vibaya

🆘 If you've thought about hurting yourself, please read thisIkiwa umewahi kufikiria kujidhuru, tafadhali soma hiiIkiwa umewahi kufikiria kujidhuru, tafadhali soma hii

Sometimes unkind treatment, or just feeling worthless or unheard, can lead to thoughts of hurting yourself, or thoughts of suicide. If that's ever happened to you, it doesn't mean something is wrong with you — it's a sign to get help, not a shame to hide. Please tell a trusted adult, or call one of the numbers below.
