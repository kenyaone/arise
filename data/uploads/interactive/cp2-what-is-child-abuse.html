🎓 Facilitator note · 5 min
This lesson may surface hard feelings — some children may recognise their own experience in it. Start by reminding the group that God loves them and has a good plan for their lives. Hold up a clean plate, then add some dirt to it: this represents people in life who might mistreat them. God's love and plan remain, even when people don't treat them with love — the plate can be cleaned, but even where scars remain, it is still useful, because God can use each person no matter what has happened to them.Somo hili linaweza kuibua hisia ngumu — baadhi ya watoto wanaweza kutambua uzoefu wao wenyewe ndani yake. Anza kwa kuwakumbusha kikundi kwamba Mungu anawapenda na ana mpango mzuri kwa maisha yao. Onyesha sahani safi, kisha uweke udongo kidogo juu yake: hii inawakilisha watu maishani wanaoweza kuwatendea vibaya. Upendo na mpango wa Mungu unabaki, hata watu wasipowatendea kwa upendo — sahani inaweza kusafishwa, na hata mahali makovu yatabaki, bado ina manufaa, kwa sababu Mungu anaweza kumtumia kila mtu haijalishi kilichompata.Hii lesson inaweza ku-surface hisia ngumu — wengine wanaweza ku-recognise uzoefu wao wenyewe ndani yake. Anza kwa kuwakumbusha group ati Mungu anawapenda na ana plan nzuri kwa maisha yao. Onyesha sahani safi, kisha uweke udongo kidogo juu yake: hii inawakilisha wasee maishani wanaoweza kuwatendea vibaya. Love na plan ya Mungu inabaki, hata wasee wasipowatendea kwa love — sahani inaweza kusafishwa, na hata pale scars zitabaki, bado ina manufaa, kwa sababu Mungu anaweza kumtumia kila msee haijalishi kilichompata.
