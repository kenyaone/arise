🔑 Your private places🔑 Sehemu Zako za Siri🔑 Sehemu Zako za SiriThese are the parts of your body that your underclothes cover. No one should ask to see or touch these — except a doctor or nurse giving needed medical care, and even then, with a trusted adult present or aware. If someone breaks this rule, it is never your fault, and you can always tell someone.Hizi ni sehemu za mwili wako zinazofunikwa na nguo za ndani. 🏠 Safe places🏠 Sehemu Salama🏠 Sehemu SalamaPlaces where other people can see you, and where you're free to leave if you want to — home, school, church, out in the open with others around.Sehemu ambako watu wengine wanaweza kukuona, na ambako uko huru kuondoka ukitaka — nyumbani, shuleni, kanisani, nje wazi na watu wengine karibu.Sehemu ambako wasee wengine wanaweza kukuona, na ambako uko huru kuondoka ukitaka — nyumbani, shuleni, kanisani, nje wazi na wasee wengine karibu.
⚠️ Unsafe places⚠️ Sehemu Zisizo Salama⚠️ Sehemu Zisizo SalamaBeing alone with someone you don't fully trust, or somewhere hidden from view. If you ever find yourself somewhere like this without wanting to be, it's okay to leave, make noise, or ask to go — you never need a "good enough" reason.Kuwa peke yako na mtu husiyemwamini kikamilifu, au mahali palipofichika. Ukijikuta mahali kama hapa bila kutaka kuwa hapo, ni sawa kuondoka, kupiga kelele, au kuomba kuondoka — hupaswi kuwa na sababu "ya kutosha".Kuwa peke yako na msee husiyemwamini kikamilifu, ama mahali palipofichika. Ukijikuta mahali kama hapa bila kutaka kuwa hapo, ni sawa kuondoka, kupiga kelele, ama kuomba kuondoka — hupaswi kuwa na sababu "ya kutosha".
🔑 Safe adult behaviour vs. unsafe🔑 Tabia Salama za Watu Wazima dhidi ya Zisizo Salama🔑 Tabia Salama za Adults dhidi ya Zisizo SalamaA safe adult helps you feel valued and safe, and respects "no." An unsafe adult tries to force, threaten, bribe, or take something from you to get their way — none of that is ever your fault, no matter what they say.Mtu mzima salama anakusaidia kujisikia una thamani na salama, na anaheshimu "hapana." Mtu mzima asiye salama anajaribu kulazimisha, kutishia, kuhonga, au kuchukua kitu kutoka kwako ili apate atakavyo — hilo si kosa lako kamwe, haijalishi wanasema nini.Adult salama anakusaidia kujisikia una value na salama, na anaheshimu "hapana." Adult asiye salama anajaribu kulazimisha, kutishia, kuhonga, ama kuchukua kitu kutoka kwako ili apate atakavyo — hilo si kosa lako kamwe, haijalishi wanasema nini.
