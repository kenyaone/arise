🎓 Facilitator note · 5 min
Pour water into the glass — this represents the kind words a young person thinks about themselves: I am loved by God, I have value, I can contribute. Then drop in a little dirt — unkind thoughts: I deserve to be hurt, I'm no good. Let the group see that words and thoughts, about ourselves or from others, can hurt. Then pour the dirty water out and add clean water back in: God's love and forgiveness can always clean away the hurt.Mimina maji kwenye bilauri — hii inawakilisha maneno mazuri kijana anayojifikiria: napendwa na Mungu, nina thamani, ninaweza kuchangia. Kisha dondosha udongo kidogo — mawazo mabaya: nastahili kuumizwa, mimi si mzuri. Acha kikundi kione maneno na mawazo, kuhusu sisi wenyewe au kutoka kwa wengine, yanaweza kuumiza. Kisha mimina maji machafu nje na uongeze maji safi: upendo na msamaha wa Mungu daima unaweza kusafisha maumivu.Mimina maji kwa glass — hii inawakilisha maneno mazuri kijana anajifikiria: nina-lovewa na Mungu, nina value, ninaweza kuchangia. Kisha dondosha udongo kidogo — mawazo mabaya: nastahili kuumizwa, mimi si mzuri. Acha group ione maneno na mawazo, kuhusu sisi wenyewe ama kutoka kwa wengine, yanaweza kuumiza.   1. Discuss peer abuse in its common forms: bullying, gang pressure, sexual pressure.
